# Villains & Velvet Scenarios

## The Assault

The Velvet Queen sends her forces against the Villains while supporting them with direct attacks and reinforcements.

### Enemy Events

| Event | Type | Copies |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Direct Assault | Leader Strike | 3 |
| Reinforcements | Scenario Twist | 5 |

### Direct Assault

The Enemy Leader attacks the Build Area using her normal Attack value.

### Reinforcements

The Minion in Area 2 attacks the Build Area using its normal Attack value. If Area 2 is empty, Reinforcements has no effect.

## The Queen's Wrath

The Queen attacks relentlessly and can command every enemy in play to launch a Full Assault.

### Enemy Events

| Event | Type | Copies |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Direct Assault | Leader Strike | 6 |
| Full Assault | Scenario Twist | 2 |

### Direct Assault

The Enemy Leader attacks the Build Area using her normal Attack value.

### Full Assault

The Enemy Leader attacks first. Every Minion currently in play then performs its full attack, including applicable abilities.

Attacks target the Build Area first. When the Build Area becomes empty, remaining Full Assault attacks target the Hand. If the Hand becomes empty during Full Assault, draw three cards and continue. Keep any cards remaining in the Hand when Full Assault ends.

## Shared Structure

Every Scenario uses 25 Minions and exactly 8 Enemy Events, for a 33-card Enemy Deck. Players may customize the Leader Strike and Scenario Twist counts, but the combined total must remain eight.
